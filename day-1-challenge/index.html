Day 01 — Self Audit

Where do you
actually stand?

Don't worry — we're starting easy. No tools to install, nothing to set up, no technical knowledge required. Day 1 is just about getting honest with yourself. Before you can move forward you need a clear picture of where you are right now. This takes 5 minutes and sets the foundation for everything this week.

Today's task
Identify your starting position. Choose which type of AI user you are right now, then answer four honest questions about your business. Your answers unlock everything that follows this week.
Step 1 — Which type are you?
Pick the one that's closest. Be honest — there's no wrong answer here.
The Dabbler Too basic
You've used ChatGPT to write an email or summarise something. That's where it ends. You know AI exists but haven't gone deeper than the surface.
The Deep Diver Too complex
You've gone down the rabbit hole — APIs, custom models, automation stacks. Fascinating but time-consuming, with no clear return yet.
The Overwhelmed In the middle
You know AI is important, you want to use it properly, but every time you start you end up more confused than when you began. You're here because something has to change.
Step 2 — Name the gap
One area in your business where AI could help — but you have no idea where to start.
Your answer
Step 3 — Honest inventory
Four quick questions. Write your instinctive answers — don't overthink these.
How many hours a week go on repetitive tasks you could hand off?
What takes the most time for the least return in your business?
If you could automate one thing tomorrow, what would it be?
What would it change if that one thing ran itself?
Paste into a notes app, Google Doc, or anywhere you keep your work.

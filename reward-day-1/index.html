The businesses making real money with AI do the opposite. They identify the highest-value area first — then find the tool built specifically for that job. What you're looking at is a map of those areas, by business type.

Use this to understand where the opportunity is in your world. The next four days of the challenge will show you how to evaluate it.

The pattern across every business type

The areas that work are always the same three categories
Every profitable AI use case in every business type above falls into one of three buckets: content and communication (writing, designing, producing), automation and operations (follow-ups, scheduling, admin), or customer experience (support, personalisation, retention). If an opportunity doesn't fit into one of these three, it's almost certainly not where you should start.
The size of your business doesn't determine the size of the opportunity
A solo trader using AI for follow-up automation can have the same infrastructure as a company with 20 people — and often better, because they move faster. The map above applies whether your business does £10k a year or £1m. The tools scale with you.
The opportunity is still wide open — but the window is narrowing
In most UK industries, fewer than 10% of businesses are using AI in a meaningful way. That gap is the opportunity. The businesses implementing now will be the standard-setters in their category inside 18 months. Everyone else will be catching up.

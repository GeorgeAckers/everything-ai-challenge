E-commerce — content production Read source ↗
Adore Me — from 20 hours to 20 minutes per product batch
Adore Me, a direct-to-consumer lingerie brand, needed to scale content production across product descriptions, multilingual website copy, and stylist notes without expanding headcount. They built role-specific AI agents using Writer's AI Studio — one for SEO product descriptions, one for Spanish translations, one for stylist note drafts. The results were significant: product description generation dropped from 20 hours per batch to 20 minutes, stylist note writing time fell by 36%, and their Mexico market launch timeline shrank from months to 10 days. They also recorded a 40% increase in non-branded SEO traffic as a direct outcome.
20 hrs → 20 mins per batch 36% less time on stylist notes +40% non-branded SEO traffic
The lesson for your business: You don't need to do everything yourself. Identify one repeatable content task — product descriptions, email templates, captions — and train an AI on your existing examples. The batch efficiency gains are immediate and compound over time.
Service business — customer email handling Read source ↗
Octopus Energy — AI-assisted emails outperform human ones on satisfaction
Octopus Energy, the UK's largest energy supplier, began rolling out AI to assist with customer email responses in early 2023. Within months, AI was helping draft over 40% of all customer communications. The outcome surprised even their own team: emails assisted by AI achieved an 80% customer satisfaction rating, compared to 65% for responses written by human agents alone. CEO Greg Jackson noted openly that the AI was outperforming people on the metric that matters most — customer happiness. The company handled the workload equivalent of roughly 250 additional staff through the AI system, without replacing existing employees.
80% satisfaction (AI) vs 65% (human) 40%+ of emails AI-assisted Equiv. of 250 staff handled
The lesson for your business: AI doesn't just save time — in the right context, it can actually improve the quality of output your customers receive. Start with the most formulaic, high-volume communications and let AI draft. Humans review. The satisfaction data follows.
Fintech — customer service automation Read source ↗
Klarna — 2.3 million conversations handled in 30 days, resolution time cut 82%
In February 2024, Klarna launched an AI customer service assistant built with OpenAI. In its first month, it handled 2.3 million customer conversations — work equivalent to roughly 700 full-time agents — and automated 67% of all customer interactions. Resolution time dropped from 11 minutes to under 2 minutes. The company projected $40 million in annual cost savings as a result. It's worth noting the full picture: by 2025, Klarna acknowledged it had leaned too hard into AI-only support and began reintroducing human agents for complex cases. The lesson isn't "automate everything" — it's that AI handles volume brilliantly while humans handle nuance. The hybrid model is the one that works.
2.3M conversations in 30 days 11 min → under 2 min resolution 67% of chats automated
The lesson for your business: Tier-1, repeat, high-volume queries are where AI wins. Complex, emotional, or relationship-critical conversations stay human. The businesses making it work aren't replacing people — they're redeploying them to the work that actually needs them.

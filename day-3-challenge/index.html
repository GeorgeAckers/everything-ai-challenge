Memory Import — bring your context into Claude
Transfer your business information so Claude knows who you are from day one
If you've been using ChatGPT, you've likely built up custom instructions — your business name, your tone, how you like responses formatted. You don't need to rebuild that from scratch. Paste it into Claude once and it carries that context into every conversation automatically.
How to do it
  1. Go to claude.ai and sign in (free account is fine)
  2. Click your profile icon → Settings
  3. Find Capabilities and click "Start Import" — copy the prompt provided and paste it into ChatGPT
  4. ChatGPT will generate a summary of your custom instructions — copy that and paste it back into Claude
  5. Save — Claude now knows who you are.

Gmail Connector — Claude in your inbox
Find emails, summarise threads, and draft replies without leaving Claude
Once connected, you can ask Claude to find specific emails, summarise what's outstanding with a client, or draft a reply in your tone — all without opening Gmail. It uses OAuth — the same secure standard every major app uses with Google. Claude does not store your emails.
How to do it
  1. In Claude, go to Settings then Connectors
  2. Click Customise, then "Add connector"
  3. Search for Gmail and select it
  4. Sign in with Google and grant permissions
  5. Test it: "Summarise the last 5 unread emails in my inbox"
  6. Then try: "Draft a reply to the most recent email from [name] — brief and professional"

Calendar Connector — manage your time with AI
Ask Claude what's in your week, find gaps, and handle scheduling
The calendar connector turns Claude into a genuine time management tool — not just a chat assistant. It reads your actual schedule and gives you specific, useful answers based on what's really in your week.
How to do it
  1. Back in Settings → Integrations, find Google Calendar
  2. Connect it the same way as Gmail — same OAuth, same security
  3. Try: "What does my week look like? Where are the gaps?"
  4. Then: "Am I free on Thursday afternoon?"
  5. Then: "I need a 45-minute call this week — what's my best slot?"

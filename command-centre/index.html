Quick-start prompt cheatsheet
Copy and use these immediately in Claude
5 starter prompts
Content repurposing
Take this [blog post / video transcript / LinkedIn post] and turn it into: 5 LinkedIn posts (100–150 words each, single point, ends with a question), 1 email newsletter (300 words, conversational), and 3 short captions for Instagram. Match my tone — direct, no fluff, no hype.
Meeting transcript → action items
Here's a meeting transcript. Give me: (1) a 3-sentence summary of what was discussed, (2) every decision made, (3) all action items with owners and deadlines, (4) anything that needs follow-up but wasn't resolved. Be precise and concise.
Brand voice setup
Here are 3 pieces of content I've written that I'm proud of: [paste examples]. Study the voice, tone, sentence length, and word choices carefully. From now on, when I ask you to write anything, match this style exactly. Don't summarise — write in this voice.
Lead follow-up sequence
Write a 4-message follow-up sequence for a lead who received a quote but hasn't replied. Message 1 (Day 1): warm check-in. Message 2 (Day 3): add value — share one relevant insight or resource. Message 3 (Day 7): gentle scarcity or soft deadline. Message 4 (Day 14): graceful close. Tone: professional, warm, never pushy.
SOP from your process
Here's a process I do regularly: [describe it step by step in plain language]. Turn this into a clean, structured Standard Operating Procedure that anyone on my team could follow without asking me a single question. Include what to do, how to do it, and why each step matters.
